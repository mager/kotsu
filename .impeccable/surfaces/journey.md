# Journey map

Mode: Operate / Experience. A complete beginner Japanese course presented as an expedition. The user requested a complete redesign and delegated the creative direction (“go wild”).

## Direction contract
THESIS: Choose your next path, complete every branch. The graph is the navigation, not a dashboard decoration.
OWN-WORLD: A cobalt expedition atlas, cool white ground, ink-blue paths, coral location markers, and green completed stamps. Japanese letterforms are the landmarks. Compact sans-serif controls and a large, blunt heading.
STORY: Begin with five sounds; choose between unlocked routes; practise in short sessions; return to fill the map; join points require every incoming branch.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact navigation, a short invitation, then a large connected route map with chapter index on the left and selected stop details on the right. The first stop and its two possible next routes are visible. Primary action lives in the stop panel.
FORM: An interactive directed acyclic journey map, chosen from the user's explicit request. Code-led implementation: geometry and actual progression define the surface. No image comp. Signature interaction: selecting a station reveals its dependencies and next routes; earned progress fills the routes.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
